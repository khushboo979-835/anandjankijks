Holistic Development Programs for Real Impact

Women Empowerment

Silai & Sewing Skill Training Centers

Providing free tailoring, stitching, and fashion design certification to rural and underprivileged women so they can launch micro-enterprises and achieve financial independence.

4,800+ Women Certified

Education For All

Vidya Jyoti Free Child Literacy Drive

Sponsoring school fees, distributing books, bags, stationery, and providing free evening remedial tuition to children from economically disadvantaged families.

6,200+ Children Enrolled

Healthcare & Wellness

Free Mega Health & Eye Screening Camps

Organizing free general health examinations, eye checkup camps with free spectacles and medicine distribution, and maternal health awareness workshops in remote villages.

150+ Camps Conducted
Support This Program
Community Welfare

Winter Blanket & Ration Distribution

Distributing warm blankets during peak winter, emergency dry ration food kits to elderly citizens, and extending emergency relief support during natural adversities.

12,000+ Kits Distributed

Green Village Tree Plantation Drives

Planting thousands of native fruit-bearing and shade trees across rural schools and community grounds, alongside cleanliness and water conservation workshops.

18,500+ Saplings Planted
