About Anand Janki Jankalyan Samiti

Transforming Communities Through Grassroots Action & Compassion

Anand Janki Jankalyan Samiti is a registered non-profit organization established under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860. Operating with deep grassroots presence across 10 states in India, we work tirelessly to uplift marginalized women, underprivileged children, and economically weaker families.

Founded with a vision of social equality and sustainable community development, our organization operates free vocational training centers (Silai & Craft Kendra), rural literacy drives, free medical health checkups, and disaster relief programs.

Under the leadership of President Rajesh Kumar, we have successfully created opportunities for thousands of women to earn respectful livelihoods and facilitated schooling for children who previously lacked access to basic education.

Our Mission

To eradicate poverty and inequality by providing rural women with employable vocational skills, ensuring quality education for every child, and delivering accessible healthcare to the underserved.

Our Vision

A self-reliant, enlightened society where every woman is economically independent, every child thrives in school, and vulnerable communities live with dignity and good health.

Core Values

Integrity, grassroots transparency, empathy, female empowerment, inclusive development, and unwavering commitment to public service.

Pan-India Operational Footprint

Impacting Lives Across 10 States in India

Our dedicated volunteers and field coordinators actively conduct skill development, education, and healthcare programs across these vibrant states.

New Delhi

National Head Office, Youth Skill Centers & Slum Child Literacy Drives.

Registered Head Office (Prayagraj), Silai Kendras & Mega Health Camps.

Uttarakhand

Hill Region Relief, Eco-Awareness & Remote Health Checkup Drives.

Bihar

Rural Literacy, Tailoring Training Batches & Blanket Distributions.

Odisha

Coastal Community Livelihood Support & Disaster Relief Drives.

Chhattisgarh

West Bengal

Assam
